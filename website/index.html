Six kilobytes of math.

morphicons solves the optimal similarity between two shapes in closed form (2D Procrustes): if a pair is congruent under rotation, it rotates; if not, it morphs in the aligned frame. Nobody declares rotation groups by hand. Springs are interruptible, corners stay sharp at rest, and the core never touches the DOM, so every Flutter surface is a first-class driver.

Icons are consumed as data, not widgets: a d string or Lucide IconNode, structurally typed. No adapters, no per-library setup.
