## 1.0.0

- Stable 1.0.0 release, ready for pub.dev.
- `MorphIcon` widget: uncontrolled, controlled (`MorphIcon.controlled`) and
  imperative (`MorphIconState.morphTo` / `.set`) modes.
- `MorphPainter` with canonical snap on settle — resting icons are
  pixel-identical to static ones.
- `MorphScheduler`: one shared `Ticker` steps every active spring.
- `size`, `strokeWidth`, `color`, `semanticLabel`, overridable viewBox,
  `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` support, first-frame-is-final-shape.
